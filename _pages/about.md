I received my Ph.D. in Informatics from SOKENDAI (National Institute of Informatics, Tokyo) in September 2026, after my bachelor's and master's degrees at the University of Tokyo.

My doctoral research measured how Internet scanners discover and probe IPv6 networks, using a darknet–honeynet telescope that I operated for nearly three years.

I am now building a self-driving laboratory (SDL) for UAV aeroacoustics, automating drone acoustic experiments end to end.

**Research interests:** network security and measurement, high-performance networking (P4, RDMA, eBPF), systems for AI, AI for Science, and embodied AI.
